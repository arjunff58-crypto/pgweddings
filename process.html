How We Work

The PG Experience

From the first hello to the final delivery, we ensure a seamless and enjoyable process.

1. Enquiry

You reach out to us via website form, WhatsApp, or phone. We check our availability and send you our customized pricing details.

2. Consultation

We connect for a coffee or a video call to understand your vision, your story, and exactly what you expect for your big day.

3. Planning

We plan the shoots, coordinate with your event planners for the lighting, and discuss timelines to ensure we don't miss any vital moments.

4. Shooting

Our unobtrusive team captures the emotions, the grandeur, and the tiny details beautifully without interrupting the flow of your wedding.

5. Editing

Our post-production magic begins. We meticulously select, color-grade, and edit the photos and cinematic films to near perfection.

6. Delivery

You receive your online gallery and cinematic films. We also design premium lay-flat albums that serve as family heirlooms.

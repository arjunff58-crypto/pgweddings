About PG Weddings

Capturing Stories with Heart

PG Weddings focuses on creating personalized wedding experiences through storytelling. We blend into your celebrations, capturing the fleeting, unscripted moments that truly embody your love.

Based out of Shoranur, Kerala, we proudly shoot weddings locally and travel across India and Dubai to craft visual poetry for our couples.

Our approach is simple: We want you to feel the emotions from your special day every time you look at our photos or watch your cinematic film. Your romance, your family, your joy — preserved forever.
